College Life On the first day of college, I went to college without knowing where I was supposed to go. Someone I knew told me to go to the third floor, so I went there and saw a classroom with “1st Year BCA” written on it. I thought that was my class and went inside. I didn’t know anyone there, and everyone was a stranger to me. I quietly went and sat at a table. After some time, the BCA department HOD, along with the other faculty members, came to our class and gave us many instructions. Then our class sir came in and asked everyone to introduce themselves by telling their names and a little about themselves. I was very nervous and scared, but somehow I managed to introduce myself. After that, the first day of college came to an end. Day by day, our classes started regularly. Slowly, I made a few friends, but initially, I wasn’t very close with anyone. I didn’t really like college at first, so I started taking a lot of leave. Then the exams came, and unfortunately, I got arrears in some subjects. As time passed, however, I slowly started getting comfortable with my class, and my friends also became closer to me. My main friends were Lokesh, Sam, Deepak, Mahesh, Keshav, Joy, and Ragu. Once our group became close, I almost stopped focusing on studies. College life became more about entertainment, fun, and spending time together. Slowly, even the lecturers became familiar with us, and that made college life even more enjoyable. Then we entered our second year. Our second year was completely different. We used to play games in class, avoid doing our work, skip classes, go outside for tea, eat biryani, and come back late to class. We also started noticing girls and trying to talk to them. One day, Sam and Deepak went to the Science Block looking for a girl. While they were going there, they got caught by a mam. Somehow, they escaped from her and went to another block to continue looking for her. Mahesh wasn’t very interested in girls, so he usually stayed in the classroom and used his mobile phone. As time went on, we started skipping more classes and stopped listening to what the teachers told us. Slowly, we also became friends with a group of six girls. Our two groups started talking and spending time together, and eventually, we all became very close. By the end of the second year, everyone was having a great time together. Then we entered our third year. When third year started, we felt a little sad because we knew that this would be our final year. We realized that after this year, all of us would graduate and go our separate ways. We were given our final-year syllabus, and life continued as usual. Then Sports Day came, and we all had a lot of fun together. After completing the fifth semester, we entered our final semester. Just like the previous semesters, the final semester was also filled with both happiness and sadness. But this time, we started focusing more on our studies because we knew that after the final semester, there would be no more college life. Finally, the final-semester exam dates were announced. We all went and wrote our exams. When the last exam was finally over, we came out of the exam hall. Everyone started taking photos and spending the last few moments together. We were all happy that we had completed college, but at the same time, we were sad because we knew that we were going to separate. After the exams, both our groups went together to eat biryani. Then we dropped everyone off one by one and finally went back home. And that was the end of our college life — a journey that started with strangers, slowly turned into friendships, and finally became a collection of memories that we will never forget.
